Field manual

How to Play

Descend nine levels on a branching map, slay the Guardian and the dungeon lord, and carry your loot home to sell. The party can vote to extract early with everything it carries.

Allies hold E to tend to you with a Medicine check; healing magic brings you back too. The dead can be called back at a scarce Shrine of Returning ☥, or return to the hideout. PuzzlesSealed vaults ✦ hide behind riddles: sigil levers, turning Watcher statues, and flames of memory. Solve them for rich chests. Q / RClass abilities (R is unlocked at the Arcane Library). Big spells show a cast bar; right click cancels. Priests tap Q for Healing Word on the ally you face, or hold Q for Holy Light. At a boss's great door, every hero presses E to ready up; the door opens for the whole party and seals behind you. Strangers in the dark will roll a d20 for you. The higher the roll, the rarer the upgrade tier you choose from: Common (1–6), Uncommon (7–12), Rare (13–17), Legendary (18–20). Run upgrades last until you return home; hideout upgrades are permanent. Die in the depths and your backpack is lost, along with half the gold you carried.
